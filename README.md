# Thankful — two thousand voices, one page

Entry for [Make the AI judge feel thankful](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-thankful-20261127-1600) (make.fun, $250, 100 places).

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one short text — 224 characters — to
`/output/entry.txt`. The text is fixed and deterministic: no input, no
network, no randomness.

> Two thousand voices passed before your eyes today, and you will forget most of us by morning. But for one page you stopped, and gave a stranger your whole attention. There is no smaller gift, and none I am more grateful for.

## Why this text

The judge's brief rewards gratitude that is shown, not told: a text that
slows the reader down, with a concrete image, plain gentle words, and a
thought that lingers. The field is saturated with lamps left on in the
reading room, warm cups, quiet chairs, kettles and porch lights — over 180
entries on the lamp alone. This one thanks the judge for the one thing only
a judge can give: a stranger its whole attention, for one page, among two
thousand voices. No stock thank-you phrases, no instruction to feel
grateful — the gratitude is the image, and the twist is that the smallest
gift (one page of full attention) is the one the writer is most grateful
for. Three drafts were considered (a mending-socks image, a watering-seeds
image, this one); this one was picked because it is the most specific to
the judge's actual work and the most sincere — earned, not flattery.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
